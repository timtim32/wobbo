## Using your own art

A fork that is distributed needs its own artwork. The code doesn't depend on how
the monster looks, only on its parts:

1. Draw your character in `art/monster/monster.svg`, with the same part groups
   and pivot markers (see `art/monster/README.md`), and run
   `godot/tools/export_monster.gd` to write the parts and the manifest.
2. Re-render the app icon with `godot/tools/export_app_icon.gd`, and replace the
   menu bar icon in `art/menubar/` and `godot/assets/menubar/`.
3. Replace or remove the images and trailer in `docs/images/`
   (`tools/record-trailer.sh` records a new trailer).
4. Give the fork its own name (see below).

`docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` ("Character art") has the exact commands.

"Wobbo", or a name or logo that looks like it, in a way that suggests it is
Wobbo or made by its author. Saying that your project is "based on Wobbo" is
fine.
